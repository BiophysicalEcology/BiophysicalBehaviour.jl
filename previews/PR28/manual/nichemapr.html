For NicheMapR users ​

In NicheMapR behaviour is inside the models it belongs to: the ectotherm model moves its animal between shade, height and depth within the Fortran program ECTOTHERM, and endoR has a thermoregulatory loop around its heat budget. Here the heat budget is in HeatExchange.jl, see its page for NicheMapR users, and everything the animal does about it is in this package.

What is different ​

The loop over hours is the user's. ectotherm takes a microclimate and returns tables for the whole period. Here thermoregulate does one hour, and the loop that calls it is a few lines, see Get started. The only things carried from hour to hour are the depth of the previous hour and whether the animal has yet been active today.

Capabilities are flags. Whether an animal seeks shade, climbs, burrows, changes colour, pants or orients to the sun is each a field of EctothermBehavioralLimits.

Every graded response has the same form. In NicheMapR each has its own trio of names, such as AK1, AK1_MAX and AK1_INC. Here each is a SteppedParameter with current, reference, max and step.

The controller is a value. The order of responses is one choice of AbstractControlStrategy among others.

An endotherm can choose where to be. endoR is given one environment. Here an endotherm can be given the AvailableEnvironments of an ectotherm, and selects among them before it thermoregulates physiologically.

How closely they agree ​

The tests of the package compare both models with NicheMapR.

For the ectotherm, the microclimate tables of NicheMapR are read in as the available environments, and hourly body temperature, shade, depth and activity are compared for four combinations of behaviours.

For the endotherm, A mammal across air temperatures computes the comparison on the page: the thresholds at which each response begins, and the value it reaches, match endoR_devel across 0 to 50 °C, and metabolic rate agrees within a few per cent. The differences are those of the heat budget, described in the documentation of HeatExchange.jl, and of where each loop stops within its tolerance.

example_respiration_pars leaves the exhaled air at lung temperature. NicheMapR's default, DELTAR = 0, has it leave at air temperature, and the comparison uses DELTAR = 100 to match.
