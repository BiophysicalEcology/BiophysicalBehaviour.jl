States, thresholds and traits ​

The documentation of HeatExchange.jl sets out why the things it computes are not traits. Body temperature is a state variable. Metabolic rate and water loss are processes. Each belongs to an organism in a place at a time. What can be a trait is a particular value of a state or process at which something happens to the organism, or at which it acts: a threshold.

This package is where thresholds live. A heat budget returns a body temperature of 60 °C without comment. The model of behaviour says that 60 °C will not do, and what the organism does instead.

Four kinds of term ​

A model of an organism as a dynamical system has four kinds of term (Kearney et al. 2021). Behaviour adds to each.

TermIn HeatExchange.jlAdded here
state variablecore, skin and surface temperatureswhere the organism is: shade, height, depth; what it is doing: Resting, Basking, Active
processheat flows, metabolic rate, water lossnone: the processes of the heat budget, evaluated in the state behaviour has chosen
environmental variableair, ground and sky temperatures, wind, humidity, radiationthe set of environments within reach, see AvailableEnvironments
parameterthe properties of the organismthe range over which each can be changed, and the thresholds at which it is

Threshold traits of an ectotherm ​

For an ectotherm the thresholds are body temperatures, fields of EctothermBehavioralLimits:

julia
limits = example_ectotherm_behavioral_limits()
ThresholdValueWhat happens there
critical_temperature_min6.0 °Cbelow it the animal cannot move; it retreats if it can
emerge_temperature_min15.0 °Cthe body temperature at which it can leave its retreat
basking_temperature_min17.5 °Cabove it the animal may be out basking, but not yet foraging
active_temperature_min24.0 °Cthe lower bound of the range for activity
target_temperature30.0 °Cthe preferred temperature: above it, the animal begins to respond to heat
active_temperature_max34.0 °Cthe upper bound of the range for activity
critical_temperature_max40.0 °Cabove it the animal cannot move; used when choosing a depth to retreat to

These are the CT_min, T_RB_min, T_B_min, T_F_min, T_pref, T_F_max and CT_max of the NicheMapR ectotherm model (Kearney and Porter 2020). They are properties of the animal: measured in a thermal gradient or observed in the field, independent of the weather, with dimensions of temperature.

Together they divide the state variable into bands, and the band decides the consequence:

An hour in which the animal can be active is an hour in which it can feed. The count of such hours is a main output of a mechanistic niche model, and it comes from two thresholds applied to a computed state.

Threshold traits of an endotherm ​

For an endotherm the thresholds are mostly limits on processes and parameters, fields of ThermoregulationLimits:

julia
limits = example_thermoregulation_limits()
ThresholdValueRole
minimum_heat_flow77.62 Wthe least heat the animal can produce: the reference of the controller
core_temperature.reference37.0 °Cthe core temperature that is defended
core_temperature.max39.0 °Cthe highest core temperature tolerated
flesh_conductivity.max2.8 W K^-1 m^-1flesh conductivity at full vasodilation
axis_ratio_factor.max5.0the most elongated posture
panting.pant.max10.0the greatest multiple of resting ventilation
skin_wetness.max1.0the greatest fraction of the skin that can be wet

The minimum metabolic rate is a threshold on a process: a solution of the heat budget below it is the signal that the animal must act. The others are the ends of the ranges over which a parameter can be changed.

Plastic parameters ​

A parameter in HeatExchange.jl has one value. Many are not constant in a living animal: fur is raised and flattened, skin is wetted, blood is sent to the skin. A SteppedParameter describes a parameter the organism can change:

julia
limits.flesh_conductivity
SteppedParameter{Quantity{Float64, 𝐋 𝐌 𝚯^-1 𝐓^-3, Unitful.FreeUnits{(K^-1, m^-1, W), 𝐋 𝐌 𝚯^-1 𝐓^-3, nothing}}, Quantity{Float64, 𝐋 𝐌 𝚯^-1 𝐓^-3, Unitful.FreeUnits{(K^-1, m^-1, W), 𝐋 𝐌 𝚯^-1 𝐓^-3, nothing}}}(0.9 W K^-1 m^-1, 0.9 W K^-1 m^-1, 2.8 W K^-1 m^-1, 0.1 W K^-1 m^-1)
FieldMeaning
currentthe value now. A state, changed by the controller
referencethe value in the resting or heat-conserving condition
maxthe limit of the response
stepthe increment by which a rule-based controller changes it

reference and max are traits. They bound the plasticity of the parameter and can be measured: the flesh conductivity of a vasoconstricted and a vasodilated limb, the depth of a flattened and a raised coat. current is a state. step belongs to the numerical method, not the animal, though it should not be finer than the animal's own control.

So a parameter of the heat budget becomes, here, a state with trait-valued bounds. In that sense physiological and behavioural thermoregulation are the same thing: both move a parameter of the physics between limits that are properties of the organism.

What is not a threshold trait ​

  • Critical air temperatures. The lower and upper critical temperatures of an endotherm, and its thermoneutral zone, are air temperatures. They belong to the environment, and hold only for the wind, humidity, radiation and posture of the measurement (Kearney et al. 2021). Here they are outputs, see A mammal across air temperatures.

  • Not to be confused with critical body temperatures. The critical thermal limits of an ectotherm are body temperatures, and are traits. The difference is whether the value is a property of the state of the organism or of the conditions that produce it.

  • Patterns of behaviour. Hours of activity, the depth of a burrow used, and the shade selected are states, however often they are reported as traits. This package computes them.

The four classes of functional trait ​

In the classification of Kearney et al. (2021):

ClassIn BiophysicalBehaviour.jl
parameterthe reference and max of each SteppedParameter; the cost of panting; the weights of the optimisation, which state the relative cost of each response
thresholdthe body temperatures of EctothermBehavioralLimits; minimum_heat_flow and the maximum core temperature of ThermoregulationLimits
modelthe thermal strategy, Ectotherm or Endotherm; the activity period; the capability flags can_climb, can_retreat_underground, can_seek_shade, can_pant and the rest; the AbstractThermoregulationMode; which part of the body holds the lungs
estimationpreferred temperatures from a thermal gradient, field body temperatures of active animals, evaporative water loss against air temperature in a metabolic chamber: observations from which thresholds and limits are estimated, given the conditions of measurement

The model traits are types and flags, on which methods are chosen. The parameter and threshold traits are the data of a limits struct, see Parameters. The states are what thermoregulate returns.
