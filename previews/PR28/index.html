🎛️ Behaviour as control

The heat budget is the plant, body temperature the state, a threshold trait the setpoint, and thermoregulation the controller.

🦎 Ectotherms

Emerge, bask, change colour and posture, seek shade, climb and retreat underground, hour by hour through a microclimate.

🐇 Endotherms

Raise and flatten fur, uncurl, vasodilate, let core temperature rise, pant and sweat, in a fixed order until the heat budget balances.

📉 Optimisation

Or state the costs and let IPOPT, with derivatives from Enzyme, find the combination of responses.

🐕 Bodies of many parts

A trunk, head and limbs with their own physiology, one core and one pair of lungs.

🌡️ Thresholds are the traits

Body temperature is a state. The temperatures at which an animal acts are its traits.

↔️ Gradients and control

Heat flows down gradients of potential. Behaviour follows gradients of information, between a state and its target.

Manual ​

BiophysicalBehaviour.jl computes what an organism does to regulate its body temperature. It takes the heat budget of HeatExchange.jl and wraps a controller around it: the organism changes its position, its posture or its physiology, the heat budget is solved again, and the result is compared with a target. The package could as well have been called BiophysicalControl.jl, see Behaviour as control.

It is part of the BiophysicalEcology ecosystem for mechanistic niche modelling. The bodies come from BiophysicalGeometry.jl, the heat budgets from HeatExchange.jl, and the environments to choose between from Microclimate.jl. See the Introduction for the design of the package and For NicheMapR users for its origin in NicheMapR.
